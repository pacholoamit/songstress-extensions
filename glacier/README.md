# Glacier

Cold, clear and quiet: smoky blue-black with an ice-mint accent, and frost for the day. An original Songstress theme, made for Songstress: one accent on deep, tinted neutrals, as Songstress's own look is.

Hairlines are the words' colour at a low alpha, so they read on the stage, on rows and on menus alike. What a look leaves out, Songstress fills from its own colours (sdk/SPEC.md, section 12).

These were moved from the first sketch to read where the apps draw them:

| Look | Token | Sketch | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glacier, Light | `accent` | `#0b7a70` | `#00756b` | 4.5:1 with room, on the stage, rows |
| Glacier, Light | `accent.fill` | `#0b7a70` | `#00756b` | the accent it is |
| Glacier, Light | `danger` | `#bf3a33` | `#bc3831` | 4.5:1 with room, on the stage, rows, menus |

The full player follows the theme, as every screen does: the look showing, its words on its own stage laid over the cover, held to the rules there. Wrapped, share cards, the first run and supporters' medals stay Songstress's own, as they do under every theme.
